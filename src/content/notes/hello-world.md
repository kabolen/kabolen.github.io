---
title: Starting over
date: 2026-08-01
excerpt: Why I rebuilt this site from scratch instead of just patching the old one.
---

I often struggle with the idea of "throwing out" something I've created, as opposed to iterating on it endlessly. It 
almost feels cruel to discard what I spent so much time building, as if it had a soul of its own, however this is a 
situation where I believe that is the right choice. 

I felt it would take much longer to change aspects of the previous version rather than simply starting with a clean 
slate. I've also improved as a developer since then, and recreating my website is a good way to gauge where I'm 
at now, to figure out where I should be focusing my efforts.

## What changed

- Content kept separate from layout, so updating it doesn't mean touching code
- One typeface *pairing*, used consistently, instead of using one typeface with differing weights
- A more "refined" color scheme
- Landing page featuring more than just a paragraph of rambling
